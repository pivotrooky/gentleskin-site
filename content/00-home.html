What it does

Today

Everything fits on one screen. Your body parts are tiles: tap one and type how many. If you'd rather time it, enter minutes instead, or both. Rings fill toward your daily target. The spots you use less sit behind the +, and you can add your own.

What was going on

When you log, tap what was happening: bored, stressed, tired, at the mirror, on your phone, in bed, or one you add. It can also ask how aware you were. You choose which questions, or none.

Urges

When you feel the urge, open the wave and keep a finger on the water, or pop kelp pods. You choose how long each one lasts. Each urge you get through shows on your chart. The urge screen is free, with or without a subscription.

What helped

When an urge passes, tap what got you through it: a fidget, gloves, leaving the room, breathing, moisturizer, talking to someone, or one you add. Patterns shows what works for you.

Patterns

One chart of your week, your month, or everything. Put a finger on it to read any day. Your daily target shows as a line. Under the chart are your days one by one, with a calendar for older ones.

Skin photos

If you want, add a photo when you log, or on any day from Patterns, and compare how a spot changes over time. The photos stay on your phone. Off unless you turn it on.

Lock Screen

One widget shows how long it's been since your last pick. Another logs a pick with one tap on a part, without opening the app.

Therapist report

A printable PDF with totals, the chart, body parts, time of day, how aware you were, what was going on and what helped. Pick a week, a month, three months, everything, or since your last session.

Check-ins are off unless you turn them on. Then they come on the days you choose, each day at its own time.

Gentleskin is in English and Spanish.

There are no streaks. The app remembers your longest time between picks, and a bad day doesn't reset anything.

Questions

How much does Gentleskin cost?

The first 7 days are free. After that it's $29.99 a year or $5.99 a month in the US, and prices vary by region. The app reminds you two days before the trial ends. The urge screen works without a subscription.

Is it only for skin picking?

It was made for skin picking, but you can use it for other body-focused repetitive behaviors too, like nail biting, cheek biting or hair pulling. You add your own spots and log them the same way.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet.

Is Gentleskin a treatment?

No. It's a tracking app, not a medical device. If you see a therapist, you can bring them the PDF report.
