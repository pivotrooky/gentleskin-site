Idea 1

Colour it in, inside the lines

For the minutes when your hands need something to do.

  • Paint with your finger. The colour stays in the shape you start in, so it never goes over a line, however you scribble.
  • Or tap, tap, tap, and each shape fills in.
  • Every page you colour is kept in a gallery. Each one opens a new colour.
